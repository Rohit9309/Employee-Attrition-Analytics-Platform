# Executive Presentation Outline

1. **Title:** Employee Attrition Analytics Platform
2. **Business problem:** attrition affects continuity, recruiting costs and team capacity; the dataset is an educational sample.
3. **Analytics workflow:** data quality → preprocessing → model comparison → evaluation → dashboard/API → monitoring.
4. **Dataset snapshot:** use actual record count and class distribution from the data.
5. **Model comparison:** populate the chart from `outputs/model_metrics.csv` after training.
6. **Key patterns:** department-level rates and demographic summaries from the dashboard; avoid causal claims.
7. **Dashboard and API:** show screenshots and the local API docs.
8. **Recommendations:** validate patterns with HR, conduct employee listening, focus on organizational interventions rather than individual targeting.
9. **Governance:** privacy, fairness, explainability, human review and monitoring.
10. **Roadmap:** Watson deployment, scheduled retraining with quality gates, calibration, drift and fairness monitoring.
